WHO WE ARE

A Purpose Built Around People.

ADNYAN Research & Educational Trust believes that every child deserves an opportunity to learn, grow and contribute to society.

Our work focuses on supporting children from underprivileged communities through educational assistance, arts, creativity and community participation.
